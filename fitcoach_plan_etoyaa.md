# Progressive Overload Rules

1. **Compound lifts (bench, row, leg press, RDL):** Add 2.5 kg when you complete ALL sets at the top rep range for 2 consecutive sessions.
2. **Isolation exercises:** Add 1 rep per session before adding weight.
3. **Running:** Never increase weekly mileage by more than 10% per week.
4. **Long run:** Add no more than 1.5 km to your long run every 2 weeks.
5. **Deload:** Every 6 weeks, reduce gym volume by 40% for 1 week. Keep running easy.
